# Privacy Policy for Al-Quran App

**Last Updated:** October 24, 2026

Thank you for choosing **Al-Quran** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, use, and security for the **Al-Quran** mobile application.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment details) to use the app.
* **No Tracking or Profiling:** We do not track, profile, or sell user activities, reading habits, or search histories.
* **No Advertising SDKs:** The application is 100% free of commercial advertisements and ad-tracking libraries.

---

### 2. Device Permissions and Usage

The application requests only the minimal permissions necessary to provide its core features:

#### a. Location (Precise and Approximate)
* **Purpose:** To calculate accurate local Islamic prayer times (Salah times) and determine the exact Qibla compass direction based on your geographic coordinates.
* **Privacy:** All calculations are performed **locally on your device**. Your location data is **never transmitted to external servers, never shared with third parties, and never stored**.

#### b. Storage and Media
* **Purpose:** To allow downloading and caching of Quranic audio recitations, translation files, and Tafseer books for offline listening and reading.
* **Privacy:** The app only accesses its own sandboxed files and cached content.

#### c. Notifications
* **Purpose:** To deliver optional local prayer time alerts (Adhan / reminders) and daily Azkar notifications if enabled by you.
* **Privacy:** Notifications are scheduled locally on your device without push notification servers tracking your device ID.

#### d. Compass and Sensors (Magnetometer / Accelerometer)
* **Purpose:** Used strictly for calibrating the interactive Qibla compass. No sensor data is logged or transmitted.

#### e. Internet Access
* **Purpose:** Required to download on-demand Quran audio files, translations, Tafseer commentaries, and check for application updates.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The app connects to public educational and Islamic open-source repositories solely to retrieve content:
* **Al-Quran Cloud / EveryAyah API:** Used strictly to fetch verified Quranic text, translations, and audio recitations. These requests are anonymous and do not contain personal identifiers.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The app contains sacred Quranic and educational content suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Data Security
Because all your bookmarks, reading progress, and preferences are stored locally in your device's internal database (SQLite / SharedPreferences), your data remains entirely under your personal control.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
